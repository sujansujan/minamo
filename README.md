# minamo — Split-View ASCII Aquarium Live Wallpaper

`split-view ascii aquarium that shifts with the sun. nothing else.`

## Features
- **Plain-Text Monospace Everything**: Both the live wallpaper and the 3-tab app UI (`[1 pond]`, `[2 set]`, `[3 about]`) are built strictly from monospace characters with ligatures disabled (`liga 0, calt 0`), square corners, 1dp hairline borders, and zero Material icons or gradients.
- **Colorful Time-of-Day Transitions**: Automatically reads your phone's clock and transitions smoothly across **Dawn** (warm espresso-rose & jade), **Noon** (sunlit Lagoon teal, coral & gold), **Dusk** (ember crimson & tangerine), and **Night** (starry indigo, crescent moon & bioluminescent cyan).
- **Random Fish Species on Every Phone Unlock**: Listening to `ACTION_USER_PRESENT` shuffles 9 single-line and multi-line ASCII fish species (`neon tetra`, `silver minnow`, `clownfish`, `blue tang`, `gold ogon`, `mandarin`, `kohaku koi`, `moorish idol`, and `veil betta`).
